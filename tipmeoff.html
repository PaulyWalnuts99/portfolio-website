Tip Me Off

A choice-based server simulator where you finally get to say what you've always wanted to say. Speak your mind, call out the guests who deserve it, and get rewarded for it.

Overview

Tip Me Off was born out of personal experience working in the restaurant industry. Anyone who has spent time in service knows the feeling — a difficult guest, a forced smile, and the words you wish you could actually say. The game is a comedic take on that tension, giving players the freedom to respond however they want and experience the real consequences of every choice.

Built in Twine over a five-week sprint with a team of five, the game places you in the role of a server during a busy Saturday dinner shift. You navigate a series of customer interactions with branching dialogue — stay professional, push back, or go full honesty. Every decision affects your tip at the end of the table, rewarding players who know how to read the room.

Mechanics
Branching Dialogue
Every customer interaction offers multiple response options — professional, neutral, or blunt. Your choices directly shape how the interaction plays out and what you walk away with.
Composure Bar
A composure meter tracks how your responses affect your mental state during the interaction. Staying professional keeps it high — but some guests make that very difficult.
Tip System
Each table ends with a tip based on how you handled the interaction. Being rude to guests who deserve it is actually rewarded — but misjudging a table will cost you.
Randomized Table Order
Scenarios are randomly generated each run so you never get the same tables in the same order. Every shift feels different, keeping the game replayable without feeling repetitive.
Design Pillars

From the start, the team established four design pillars that guided every decision — from how dialogue was written to how the tip system was balanced. Expressing these pillars clearly in the final product was the primary goal of the design process.

Pillar 01
Rudeness Must Have Consequences
Players always have the option to be rude, but actions have consequences — in the form of customer reactions and tips affected by attitude, just like real life.
Pillar 02
Believable Restaurant Scenarios
Guest complaints and requests were drawn from personal experience in the service industry to make every interaction feel grounded and authentic.
Pillar 03
Comedy Through Social Tension
Dialogue options and consequences are humorous rather than serious. Sarcasm and blunt responses drive the tone of every interaction.
Pillar 04
Customer Personality Drives Gameplay
Every customer has a distinct identity and personality that shapes how the player approaches them. Customers come from all different backgrounds to diversify the experience.
