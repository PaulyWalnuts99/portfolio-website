About Me

I'm Paul Saturno, a senior at the University of Central Florida studying Digital Media: Video Game Design, graduating May 2026. I also hold a Bachelor's in Business from UCF (2022), giving me a unique blend of creative and strategic thinking I bring to every project.

I'm passionate about designing game spaces that feel intentional — environments where every encounter, layout, and prop serves the player experience. My focus is on building levels that teach, challenge, and reward players through thoughtful design rather than hand-holding.
